## 3. Evidence gate
- [ ] Customer and payer named, or TO VERIFY per line.
- [ ] No market size, price, or cost without a source and a period,
      or a TO VERIFY label.
- [ ] Licence / regulatory requirement checked in writing, or
      TO VERIFY.
- [ ] First verification step attempted, result recorded — including
      a negative result.

## 5. What it is not
- This template does not rank, rate, or recommend the proposal.
- It does not state or imply a return, and it never carries a buy,
  sell, or hold label.
- A proposal that passes this template is testable, not proven.
