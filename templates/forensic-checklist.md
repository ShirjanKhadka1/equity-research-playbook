# Forensic Checklist — read before you trust the numbers

Tick each item, cite the page/section, and write "Pass / Flag / Not applicable"
with one line of evidence. A flag is not an accusation — it is a question to answer.

## Revenue and cash
- [ ] Revenue trend vs operating cash flow trend — do they move together?
- [ ] Receivables growing faster than sales? By how much, over how many years?
- [ ] Revenue recognised before cash collected — what are the terms?

## Profit quality
- [ ] One-off gains/losses in the year — size, and profit with them removed?
- [ ] Tax: current tax, deferred tax, prior-period adjustments — any item larger
      than normal profit? (A tax adjustment bigger than the year's profit
      changes the whole story — flag it, do not bury it.)
- [ ] Depreciation vs capex — is the asset base being maintained or run down?

## Balance sheet
- [ ] Debt: current vs non-current split — is short-term debt funding long-term assets?
- [ ] Related-party balances and transactions — size and terms?
- [ ] Auditor: who, any change, any qualification or emphasis of matter?

## Governance and disclosure
- [ ] Shareholding concentration — who controls the company?
- [ ] Dividends paid vs profit earned — sustainable, or funded by debt?
- [ ] Contingent liabilities / tax disputes disclosed — size vs equity?

## Verdict
- Clean / Watch / Flagged, with the top 3 evidence lines:
- What single additional document would resolve the biggest flag?
