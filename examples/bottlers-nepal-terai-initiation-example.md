## 1. Thesis in one paragraph

Bottlers Nepal (Terai) is a Nepal-listed beverage bottler whose audited
FY 2081/82 year looks far worse than its operating year: revenue fell
14.2% and EBIT fell 43.5%, yet operating cash flow reached a three-year
high, and the reported net loss is entirely explained by a single
Rs 763,054,705 prior-period tax adjustment (see §4). The research
question this template demonstrates is therefore not "is this cheap?"
— no valuation is built here — but "what would a reader need to check
before anyone could value it?": the tax provision's fate, whether the
FY 2082/83 quarterly recovery is durable, and whether thin trading
makes any market price a usable anchor at all.

   set by a handful of trades (as with the ledger's 2-trade closes)

3. All borrowings current — trigger: refinancing or repayment
   pressure on the NPR 2,344,466,783 all-current borrowing stack
   (FY 2081/82, per ledger) against cash of NPR 36,531,377.
4. Depressed-base error — trigger: a future valuation applies a
   multiple or a growth rate to FY 2081/82 LTM earnings without
   normalisation, overstating or understating value.
5. Stale peer quotes — trigger: a peer used as a comp has not traded
   for sessions (the ledger records BNL stale since 9 October 2026
